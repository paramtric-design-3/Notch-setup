How to get a client ID (once, about 3 minutes)
  1. Open App registrations in the Azure portal and sign in with your Microsoft account (the one you use for To Do).
  2. Click New registration. Name: WALL-E. Supported account types: Personal Microsoft accounts only. Leave Redirect URI empty and click Register.
  3. Copy the Application (client) ID and paste it in the box above.
  4. In the menu on the left open Authentication, set Allow public client flows to Yes and click Save.
  5. Back here, click Sign in. Your browser opens Microsoft's sign-in page and the code is already on your clipboard: paste it, sign in and allow access to your tasks.

If Azure asks you to create a directory or a free Azure account first, that's Microsoft's requirement for registering an app; the registration itself is free. WALL-E only asks for access to your tasks, never sees your password and keeps the sign-in encrypted on this PC.

Updates

Version …

WALL-E checks for a new version every 5 minutes and whenever your PC wakes up. It downloads updates in the background and installs them by itself straight away while WALL-E is closed (it restarts in a few seconds).

Nothing leaves this PC except weather requests (Open-Meteo), lyrics lookups (LRCLIB), album covers when the player doesn't provide one (iTunes Search), League pictures (Riot Data Dragon), OP.GG's open champion data, your To Do tasks if you connect Microsoft To Do, and update checks (GitHub). No accounts, no analytics.
